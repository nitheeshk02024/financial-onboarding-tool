WEALTH & TAX SAVINGS

First-Time Earner Tax-Saving Starter Guide

Understand Section 80C, 80D, and NPS options without financial jargon. Learn where to invest your hard-earned money to maximize legal tax deductions.

Section 80C Deduction Capacity Tracker

Statutory maximum deduction under Section 80C is ₹1,50,000 per financial year.

Max Limit: ₹1,50,000
Mandatory EPF (12% of Basic): ₹0
Remaining 80C Headroom: ₹1,50,000
Estimated Tax Saved: ₹0 /yr
📈

ELSS Mutual Funds

Recommended for Youth

Equity Linked Savings Scheme mutual funds invest in stock markets with the shortest lock-in period (3 years) among all 80C instruments.

Lock-in Period:3 Years (Lowest)
Historical Returns:12% – 15% CAGR
Risk Profile:Moderately High (Equity)
Tax on Gains:LTCG exempt up to ₹1.25L
💡 Tip for freshers: Start a monthly SIP of ₹3,000 – ₹5,000 in an ELSS index or flexi-cap fund.
🏛️

Public Provident Fund (PPF)

100% Sovereign Guarantee

Government-backed long-term savings account with sovereign capital protection and completely tax-free interest and maturity payouts (EEE status).

Lock-in Period:15 Years
Current Interest:7.1% p.a. (Compounded)
Risk Profile:Zero (Govt of India backed)
Tax Status:EEE (Exempt-Exempt-Exempt)
💡 Tip for freshers: Open a PPF account with your primary bank early to start the 15-year clock ticking.
🛡️

NPS (Sec 80CCD 1B)

Extra ₹50,000 Deduction

National Pension System allows an additional ₹50,000 tax deduction over and above the ₹1.5 Lakh 80C limit for retirement building.

Lock-in Period:Until Retirement (Age 60)
Expected Returns:9% – 12% (Market linked)
Extra Deduction:₹50,000 under 80CCD(1B)
Asset Choices:Auto Choice / Active Equity
💡 Tip for freshers: Great if your CTC is above ₹12 Lakhs and you want an extra tax shield.
🩺

Health Insurance (Sec 80D)

Up to ₹75,000 Total Cover

Premiums paid for personal health insurance (up to ₹25,000) and for senior citizen parents (up to ₹50,000) are deductible under Section 80D.

Self & Family Limit:Up to ₹25,000 / year
Senior Parents Limit:Up to ₹50,000 / year
Preventive Checkup:Up to ₹5,000 included
Benefit:Protection + Tax Reduction
💡 Tip for freshers: Never rely 100% on corporate group insurance; maintain a personal super top-up.
